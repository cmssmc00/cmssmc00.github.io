Research & academic work

Mingshi Chen

Selected research

DuoGate: Action and Context Control for Reliable Language Agents

Submitted to ICLR 2027 · Under review

A framework that couples evidence-based action control with selective context adaptation. The system checks whether actions are grounded and outcomes are supported, then uses separate gates to decide when to learn and which lessons to retain.

On AppWorld, DuoGate with MiniMax M2.7 increased mean task completion from 60.7% to 69.2% and scenario completion from 41.7% to 54.2% versus ACE over three runs.

Methods & evaluation

Implemented checks for argument grounding, authorization, outcome support, and completion evidence, with recovery directed toward identified gaps. Evaluated on AppWorld, tau2-Bench, and Terminal-Bench 2, including component and feedback ablations.

Post-training data & evaluation

Workplace Task Generation for Language-Agent Post-Training

Developing a GDPval-inspired corpus built from O*NET occupations and public workplace documents. Tasks pair realistic requests with supporting files, explicit deliverables, and criterion-level evaluation rubrics.

Pipeline details

Implemented a WorkGenesis-based pipeline with source provenance, reproducible occupation schedules, resumable parallel generation, deduplication, bounded retries, and file-integrity checks. The broader pipeline supports independent solving, rubric-based judging, defect attribution, and teacher-trajectory collection.

Agent systems & efficiency

Shared Exploration for Token-Efficient Coding Agents

Designed a task-group architecture in which related coding tasks share a persistent exploration agent while keeping planning, implementation, and evaluation separate. The goal is to reduce repeated retrieval and context-processing costs.

System & evaluation

Built reproducible task grouping using TF-IDF and cosine similarity, asynchronous evidence exchange, and task-scoped access controls. Implemented a CodeProjectEval harness with isolated outputs, official-test scoring, and request-level token accounting that counts shared exploration once per group.

Additional research · Health

Pediatric Brain Tumor Classification

Patient-level classification from whole-slide histopathology using frozen CONCH embeddings and gated-attention multiple-instance learning, alongside age and anatomical location. Audited a cohort of 255 patients and 260 usable slides, and compared hierarchical and global approaches across ten diagnostic classes.

Geographic Variation in COPD Care

Reproducible analysis of inpatient and outpatient encounter data across 44 Austin ZIP codes and 22 quarters. Studied geographic variation in recorded COPD care using observed-to-expected ratios, statistical comparisons, and sensitivity analyses.
